# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest earned on a principal amount based on the annual interest rate and the time period.

## Formula

The simple interest is calculated using the following formula:

**Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time**

Where:

- **Principal** is the initial amount of money.
- **Rate** is the annual interest rate.
- **Time** is the time period in years.

## Example

If the principal is $1,000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

**Simple Interest = 1000 × 0.05 × 2 = $100**
